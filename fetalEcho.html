What is Fetal Echocardiography?

A fetal echocardiography is a specialised ultrasound scan that examines your baby's heart in detail — including its structure, valves, chambers, and blood flow. It goes beyond a routine pregnancy scan to provide a comprehensive assessment of your baby's cardiac health before birth.

At Meera 4D Scans Madurai, we use advanced ultrasound technology to perform accurate, detailed fetal echo scans — giving every mother clarity and confidence about their baby's heart health.

Why is Fetal Echocardiography Important?

The fetal echo is one of the most important specialised pregnancy scans because:

  • Early detection of congenital heart defects
  • Assesses all four chambers of the baby's heart
  • Evaluates heart valves and blood flow
  • Identifies rhythm abnormalities before birth
  • Allows doctors to plan delivery and postnatal care in advance
  • Recommended when there is a family history of heart conditions

When is Fetal Echo Done?

The fetal echocardiography is best performed between 18 and 24 weeks of pregnancy for the most accurate cardiac assessment.

Weeks Recommended?
Before 18 weeks Too early for detailed assessment
18 – 24 weeks Ideal window for fetal echo
After 24 weeks Can be done but less optimal

At Meera 4D Scans Madurai, we recommend booking your fetal echo between 18 and 24 weeks for the most accurate cardiac assessment.

What Does Fetal Echo Check?

  • Four Chamber View — size and structure of all four heart chambers
  • Heart Valves — opening, closing, and function
  • Great Vessels — aorta and pulmonary artery assessment
  • Blood Flow — direction and velocity through the heart
  • Heart Rhythm — rate and regularity of fetal heartbeat
  • Septal Walls — detection of holes between chambers

Who Needs a Fetal Echo?

  • Family history of congenital heart disease
  • Mother has diabetes or lupus
  • Abnormal finding in routine anomaly scan
  • High NT scan measurement
  • Twin pregnancy or multiple gestation
  • Mother taking medications that may affect fetal heart

Is Fetal Echo Safe?

  • Completely safe — uses sound waves only
  • No radiation — safe for mother and baby
  • Non-invasive — no needles or discomfort
  • Takes approximately 30 to 45 minutes
  • Performed by experienced cardiac sonologists
