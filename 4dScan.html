What is a 4D Scan?

A 4D ultrasound scan is an advanced pregnancy imaging technique that captures live, moving video of your baby in the womb as opposed to standard 2D scans which produce flat, still images. The "4D" refers to three-dimensional images shown in real time (the fourth dimension being time), allowing you to see your baby yawn, stretch, smile, and move. Why Does Timing Matter for a 4D Scan?

The timing of your 4D ultrasound scan pregnancy directly affects the quality of the images. The ideal window depends on four key factors:

  • Baby's fat development subcutaneous fat fills out facial features, making them clearer on scan
  • Amniotic fluid levels sufficient fluid around the baby allows sound waves to travel clearly
  • Baby's position more space in the womb means a higher chance of a face-forward position
  • Baby's activity mid-pregnancy babies are active, giving you more chances to capture movements

Too early and the baby lacks the facial fat that defines features. Too late and the baby may be too large with reduced fluid, making clear images more difficult. Getting the timing right at Meera 4D Scans Madurai ensures the best possible experience.

Can You Get a 4D Scan in Each Trimester?

Here is a trimester-by-trimester breakdown to help you understand what a 4D ultrasound scan pregnancy looks like at each stage:

  • First Trimester (1–12 weeks) 4D scanning is not typically performed in this stage. Standard dating and viability scans are done instead.
  • Second Trimester (13–26 weeks) Anatomy scans and anomaly scans are the priority. 4D imaging can begin towards the end of this stage but results are not at their clearest.
  • Third Trimester (27–40 weeks) This is when 4D baby scans are most rewarding. The 26–32 week window within this stage gives the best combination of facial detail and available space.
