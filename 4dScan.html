What is a 4D Scan?

A 4D ultrasound scan in pregnancy is a real-time, live-video ultrasound that lets you watch your baby's movements, facial expressions, and features on screen. Unlike a standard 2D scan that produces flat, grey-scale images, a 4D scan layers thousands of 3D images per second to create a live, moving portrait of your baby — yawning, smiling, blinking, and stretching.

At Meera 4D Scans Madurai, we use the latest high-definition 4D ultrasound equipment, operated by certified sonologists — giving you the clearest, most detailed view of your baby and a keepsake you will treasure forever.

Best Time for a 4D Scan in Pregnancy

The best time for a 4D scan is between 26 and 32 weeks of pregnancy. During this window your baby has developed enough subcutaneous fat for clear facial definition, and there is still sufficient amniotic fluid for an unobstructed view. At Meera 4D Scans Madurai, our sonologists recommend 28–30 weeks as the optimal time for the highest image quality.

Gestational Age 4D Scan Quality Recommendation

Why is 26–32 Weeks the Ideal Window?

  • Baby has enough subcutaneous fat for clear, defined facial features
  • Amniotic fluid volume is optimal — maximum image clarity
  • Baby is highly active — you can watch kicks, yawns, smiles, and stretches live
  • Baby is still small enough to image in full — face and body visible
  • Gender confirmation remains reliable at this stage
  • Perfect timing for a bonding experience with your partner and family

Is 4D Scan Safe for the Baby in the Third Trimester?

Is 4D scan safe for the baby in the third trimester? Yes — absolutely. This is one of the most frequently asked questions at Meera 4D Scans Madurai, and the answer is a confident yes. A 4D ultrasound scan in pregnancy uses only sound waves — there is zero radiation, zero needles, and zero discomfort.

  • No ionising radiation — uses only reflected sound waves, just like a standard scan
  • Non-invasive — external transducer only, no discomfort
  • No known side effects for mother or baby at diagnostic power levels
  • Takes only 20–30 minutes
  • Performed by IRIA-certified sonologists following strict ALARA safety principles
  • Endorsed by ISUOG, NHS, and the American Institute of Ultrasound in Medicine (AIUM)

All 4D ultrasound scans in pregnancy at Meera 4D Scans Madurai are performed by certified sonologists using state-of-the-art equipment — following ALARA (As Low As Reasonably Achievable) safety protocols to protect both you and your baby.

How to Prepare for Your 4D Scan Appointment

Getting the clearest images depends as much on preparation as on technology. Follow these steps before you arrive at Meera 4D Scans Madurai:

1
Hydrate Well

Drink 2–3 glasses of water 30 minutes before your scan. Good hydration improves amniotic fluid clarity and gives you crisper 4D images.

2
Have a Light Snack

A small snack 20–30 minutes before the scan encourages baby activity — an active baby means better facial views and more moments to capture.

3
Wear Comfortable Clothing

Choose loose, comfortable clothes that allow easy access to your abdomen. Two-piece outfits work best.

4
Bring Your Family

Invite your partner, parents, or siblings — the 4D ultrasound scan in pregnancy is a live, shared bonding moment you will all remember.

5
Book Your Appointment in Advance
