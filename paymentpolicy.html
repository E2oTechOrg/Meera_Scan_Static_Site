Payment Policy

Payment Methods We accept cash, UPI, debit cards, credit cards, and net banking. All digital payments are processed through secure, encrypted gateways. Payment is required at the time of your scan or service.

Payment Confirmation A payment receipt will be sent via SMS or email upon successful payment. Please retain your receipt for records. Report any payment discrepancy to our team within 24 hours.

Failed Payments If a digital payment fails, contact our support team immediately with your transaction details. We are not responsible for failures caused by your bank or network issues. Genuine payment issues will be resolved promptly.

Your Rights & Data Sharing You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal information at any time. We never sell or share your data with third parties without your written consent. Contact hello@meerascans.com for any privacy concerns.
