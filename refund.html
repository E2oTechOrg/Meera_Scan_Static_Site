Cancellation & Refund Policy

Appointment Cancellation Please inform us at least 24 hours before your scheduled scan to cancel your appointment. Late cancellations or no-shows without prior notice may not be eligible for a refund. Timely notice helps us serve other patients effectively.

Rescheduling Policy Contact us at least 24 hours in advance to reschedule your appointment. We will accommodate a new time based on availability. Multiple rescheduling requests may be subject to availability and applicable fees.

Refund Eligibility Refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis. If a scan cannot be completed due to technical issues on our end, a full refund will be processed. Patient-initiated refund requests must be made within 24 hours of the appointment.

Refund Processing Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days to your original payment method. Cash refunds are issued at our centre during business hours. Contact +91 90428 83031 for any refund queries.
