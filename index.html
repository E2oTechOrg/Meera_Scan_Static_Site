Antenatal Scan

An antenatal scan is an ultrasound test during pregnancy to check the baby’s growth and health. It can detect problems early and also checks the placenta and amniotic fluid. It is safe for both mother and baby. It helps ensure a healthy pregnancy and safe delivery.

Whole Abdomen Scan

A Whole Abdomen scan is an ultrasound test that checks internal abdominal organs. It helps detect infections, stones, and other abnormalities safely without using radiation.

Learn More

Adult Echo

An Adult Echo is an ultrasound test that checks the heart’s structure and function. It helps assess heart valves, chambers, and blood flow safely without radiation.

Learn More

Fetal Interventions

Fetal Interventions are medical procedures performed during pregnancy to treat or monitor the baby before birth. They help manage complications safely and improve pregnancy outcomes.
