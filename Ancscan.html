What is an ANC Scan?

ANC stands for Antenatal Care — a series of regular medical check-ups, blood tests, and ultrasound scans performed throughout pregnancy to monitor the health of both mother and baby.

At Meera 4D Scans Madurai, our ANC scan package covers all essential pregnancy scans from the first trimester to delivery — all under one roof with experienced sonologists and same day reports.

Why is ANC Scan Important?

  • Monitors baby's growth and development at every stage
  • Detects complications early for timely medical intervention
  • Assesses placental health and amniotic fluid levels
  • Monitors maternal health including blood pressure and diabetes
  • Ensures baby's position is correct before delivery
  • Provides peace of mind throughout your pregnancy journey

Third Trimester 32 – 34 weeks Fetal well-being scan
Third Trimester 36 – 40 weeks Presentation scan — delivery planning

What Does ANC Scan Include?

  • Dating Scan — confirms pregnancy and due date
  • NT Scan — chromosomal risk assessment at 11–14 weeks
  • Anomaly Scan — detailed fetal structural examination at 18–22 weeks
  • Growth Scan — monitors baby's weight and development
  • Doppler Scan — blood flow through umbilical cord
  • Presentation Scan — baby's position before delivery

ANC Blood Tests at Meera 4D Scans

  • Urine analysis and culture

Is ANC Scan Safe?

  • All ultrasound scans are completely safe — no radiation
  • Non-invasive — no discomfort for mother or baby
  • Blood tests use minimal blood samples
  • Internationally recommended for all pregnancies
  • Performed by certified sonologists at Meera 4D Scans
