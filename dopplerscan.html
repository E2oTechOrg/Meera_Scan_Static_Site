If your doctor has recommended a Doppler scan during your pregnancy, you may be wondering what it is and why it is needed. At Meera 4D Scans Madurai, we perform advanced Doppler scans to ensure the safety and well-being of your baby throughout pregnancy.

What is a Doppler Scan?

A Doppler scan is a specialised ultrasound that measures and monitors blood flow through your baby's umbilical cord, placenta, and blood vessels. It uses sound wave technology to detect the speed and direction of blood circulation, giving doctors a clear picture of how well your baby is receiving oxygen and nutrients.

At Meera 4D Scans Madurai, we use advanced Colour Doppler technology to provide the most precise blood flow assessment — ensuring your baby's health and safety throughout pregnancy.

Why is the Doppler Scan Important?

  • Monitors blood flow to your baby through the umbilical cord
  • Assesses placental function and efficiency
  • Detects growth restriction or reduced oxygen supply early
  • Monitors high-risk pregnancies with expert precision
  • Helps doctors make timely, informed decisions for your care

When is a Doppler Scan Needed?

Condition Why Doppler is Needed
High-Risk Pregnancy Monitor blood flow closely
Reduced Fetal Movement Check baby's circulation
Growth Restriction Assess placental blood supply
Pre-eclampsia Monitor blood pressure & flow
Twin Pregnancy Monitor both babies' blood flow

What Does the Doppler Scan Measure?

  • Umbilical Artery Doppler — blood flow through umbilical cord
  • Middle Cerebral Artery (MCA) — blood flow to baby's brain
  • Uterine Artery Doppler — blood flow from mother to placenta
  • Fetal Heart Rate — cardiac function assessment

Is the Doppler Scan Safe?

  • Completely safe for mother and baby
  • Uses sound waves only — no radiation
  • Non-invasive — no needles or discomfort
  • Takes only 20 to 30 minutes
